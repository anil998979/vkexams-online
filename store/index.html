What to Check Before Purchasing a Package

  • Confirm the course and examination paper included.
  • Review the number and type of tests provided.
  • Check the package price and validity period.
  • Read the purchase and access conditions.
  • Use the student account associated with your purchase.
  • Confirm the package details before making payment.

How to Access Your Purchased Tests

  1. Visit the VK Exams website.
  2. Sign in using your student account.
  3. Open the Store and review available packages.
  4. Select the package you want to purchase.
  5. Review its details and complete the purchase process.
  6. Access the purchased resources through your account, subject to the package conditions.
