Endowment Officer Exam Preparation

VK Exams provides preparation resources for candidates preparing for Andhra Pradesh Endowment Officer recruitment. Explore important subjects relating to Hindu religious traditions, temple systems, religious institutions and the legal framework governing endowments in Andhra Pradesh.

Candidates should follow the latest official recruitment notification and prescribed syllabus while preparing for the examination. Use structured study resources and practice tests to assess subject knowledge and examination readiness.

Important Endowment Officer Preparation Topics

1. Hindu Philosophy and Religious Traditions

  • Hindu philosophy and the six Darshanas.
  • Vedic culture, Vedas and Upavedas.
  • Upanishads and Dharmashastras.
  • Important religious traditions and philosophical schools.

2. Temple Administration

  • Temple systems and Hindu religious institutions.
  • Temple administration and management.
  • Religious endowments and institutional responsibilities.
  • Duties and responsibilities relating to temple administration.

3. Andhra Pradesh Endowments Act

  • Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987 (Act 30 of 1987).
  • Important provisions relating to Endowment institutions.
  • Duties of Executive Officers and administrative provisions.
  • Sources of income and allocation of institutional funds.

4. Endowment Lands and Records

  • Basic knowledge of land records.
  • Legal provisions relating to Endowment lands.
  • Record of Rights and Pattadar Pass Books.
  • Relevant statutory provisions relating to Endowment properties.
